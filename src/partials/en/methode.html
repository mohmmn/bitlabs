Our method

A clear method, from the first conversation to production.

Each step is approved with you before we move on to the next. You always know where your project stands.

  1. 01

    First conversation

    You tell us about your project by email or phone. We ask the questions that help us understand it properly.

  2. 02

    Analysis and quote

    We analyse your needs and send you a detailed quote: scope, steps, timeline and budget.

  3. 03

    Design

    Technical architecture, user journeys and mockups: everything is approved with you before we write any code.

  4. 04

    Development

    We build in short iterations, with regular check-ins to track progress and adjust.

  5. 05

    Launch and support

    Going live, app store release if needed, then maintenance and new features.
